How to Play — Liar's Dice

The Object

Be the last one holding dice. You and Nikki each start with five dice, shaken in the cup and kept hidden.

How a Round Plays

  • Both cups shake, slam down, and stay down. Hold PEEK to lift only your cup and look at your own dice — Nikki's stay covered, and she can't see yours.
  • Players take turns bidding. A bid names how many dice at the whole table — yours and hers together — show one face: "four 5s," say. Ones are wild: every 1 counts as the bid face too.
  • On your turn, either raise or call her out. A raise is more dice, or the same count with a higher face — the picker is already set to the lowest legal raise, so PLACE BID always raises legally.
  • Think her bid is more than the table really holds? Tap LIAR! Both cups lift and every die is counted, wild ones included.

Losing Dice

If the table holds at least the bid, the bid was honest — the caller loses a die. If it falls short, the bidder loses a die. The loser opens the next round. Run out of dice and you lose the match; take all five of Nikki's and it's yours.
