How to Play — Shuffleboard

The Object

Outscore the House over 4 rounds. Your pucks are red, the House's are blue. Each round you slide all four of your pucks first, then the House answers with its four.

Shooting

  • Press on the table, pull down, and let go — the longer the pull, the faster the puck. Drag sideways while you pull to aim.
  • Pucks collide, so a well-judged shot can knock a House puck out of a scoring zone — or knock your own in deeper.

Scoring

  • A puck scores only if it stops fully past the foul line — the whole puck across the line. Hanging over the line scores nothing.
  • Where a scoring puck stops sets its points: the far red zone is 10, the blue zone is 8, the green zone is 7. Points show right on the pucks at rest.

Rounds & Winning

When both sides have shot, the round's points are added up — the R1 to R4 chips under the score keep the running story. After 4 rounds, the higher total wins the match; level totals finish dead even. NEW MATCH starts over any time.
