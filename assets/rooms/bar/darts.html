How to Play — Darts 301

The Object

Count down from 301 to exactly zero before the House does. You throw first, and turns alternate — three darts a turn, every dart counting down from your score.

Throwing

  • Your aim is the gold ring, and it drifts and wobbles around the board on its own — it never sits still.
  • Tap THROW DART (or tap the board) to loose the dart where the ring is at that moment. Timing is the whole game.

Scoring

  • A dart scores the number of the segment it lands in. The thin outer ring is the double — twice the segment. The thin middle ring is the treble — three times.
  • The green outer bull scores 25; the red bull scores 50. Off the board is a miss: 0.

Bust & Winning

Go below zero, or leave yourself exactly 1, and the turn is a BUST — the whole turn scores nothing and your score goes back to what it was when the turn began. Win by landing on exactly zero with any dart of your turn. The House plays by the same rules — busts and all. NEW LEG starts a fresh 301 any time.
