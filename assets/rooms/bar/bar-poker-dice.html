You Win the Match!

Poker Dice: the dice are card ranks — 6 is the Ace, then King, Queen, Jack, Ten, and the 9. You get up to 3 shakes; between shakes, tap a die to hold it out of the cup (tap a held die to toss it back in), or keep your hand early. Five of a Kind beats Four, then Full House, Straight (A-K-Q-J-10 or K-Q-J-10-9), Three of a Kind, Two Pair, One Pair. High dice break ties. First to 5 rounds takes the match.
