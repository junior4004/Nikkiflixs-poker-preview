How to Play — Pool

The Object

You against the House on a full north–south table: solids 1–7 or stripes 9–15, then the 8-ball. First to clear their group and sink the 8 takes the table.

Shooting

  • Press anywhere on the table and drag back, away from the cue ball — like pulling back a cue stick.
  • The gold dashed line is your shot: the cue ball travels along it. The longer the pull, the harder the shot — watch the POWER bar fill.
  • Release to shoot. Balls that reach any of the six pockets drop. Pocket one of your group and you shoot again; miss, and the House steps in.

Groups, Turns & the 8

  • You break from the south. The table starts open — the first group ball pocketed on a shot after the break sets your group (solids or stripes); the House gets the other.
  • Stripes are white balls with a wide color band; solids are all color. The tray under the table shows what each side has left.
  • Sink the cue ball and it spots right back up on the head spot, free — but your turn is over.
  • Sink the 8 early, or scratch on the 8, and you lose on the spot. Clear your group first, then the 8, and the table is yours.
