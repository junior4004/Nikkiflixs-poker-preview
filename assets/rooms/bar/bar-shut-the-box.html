How to Play — Shut the Box

The Object

You and Nikki each play a full run at a box of nine tiles, 1 through 9. Your score is the total left standing when your run ends — lower is better. Low score takes the round; first to 5 rounds wins the match.

Your Run

  1. Tap SHAKE — the cup rattles, slams, and lifts on two dice. Their total is your number.
  2. Tap standing tiles that add up to exactly that total, then tap FLIP to lay them down. A selection that doesn't add up exactly can't flip.
  3. Shake again and keep flipping. When the tiles still standing add up to 6 or less, you roll one die from then on.
  4. Your run ends when a roll comes up that no combination of standing tiles can make. Whatever is still up is your score. Flip all nine down and you've SHUT THE BOX — score 0.

Rounds

Nikki then plays her run at her own box, same rules. Lower score wins the round; a tie is a push, no point. First to 5 rounds takes the match.
