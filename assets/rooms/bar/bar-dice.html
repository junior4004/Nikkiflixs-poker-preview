How to Play — Bar Dice

The Object

Beat Nikki, the bartender, to 5 rounds. Each round you both take one turn shaking five dice — build a crew, and the two dice left over are your Cargo. High Cargo takes the round.

How a Turn Plays

  1. Tap SHAKE. The cup rattles, slams down on the bar, and lifts to show your five dice.
  2. You need a crew, in this order: a 6 is the Ship, then a 5 is the Captain, then a 4 is the Crew. A die only counts once the ones before it are aboard — a 5 rolled before the Ship just goes back in the cup.
  3. Dice that count move up into the crew slots. The rest go back under the cup and you shake again — up to 3 shakes a turn.
  4. The moment Ship, Captain and Crew are all aboard, your turn ends: the last two dice are your Cargo, and their total is your score. No crew in 3 shakes? Cargo is 0.

Scoring & Winning

Nikki takes her turn by the same rules. The higher Cargo wins the round; equal Cargo is a push and nobody scores. First to 5 rounds wins the match.
