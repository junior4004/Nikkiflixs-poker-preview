How to Play — 7 Up Down

The Object

Call where two dice land — before they're shaken. Call it right, the round is yours; first to 5 rounds wins the match.

Each Round

  1. You call first: Down — the two dice total 2 to 6. Seven — exactly 7. Up — 8 to 12.
  2. Nikki shakes the house cup — rattle, slam, lift — and the two dice settle it.
  3. Your call matches the total's band, you take the round. Miss, and the house takes it.

The Seven

Seven is the hard call — only one total out of the whole range wins it. The board keeps a glory count of every Seven rolled all match. It doesn't change the score; it's bragging rights, counted out loud.
