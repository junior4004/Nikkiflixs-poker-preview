Only poker winnings count

Monthly Won Chips adds every Gold Coin payout you receive from poker pots during the month. Purchased coins, account transfers, starting balance, and losses do not add to this ranking. That is why this total can be different from your available Gold Coin balance. This leaderboard applies to poker only — it is not a playthrough total.

Every player’s count resets to zero at the start of each month. Nothing rolls over or adds across months. Whoever finishes that month with the most Monthly Won Chips always takes first place and always receives GC 100,000 free.

The champion banner is a separate record: the highest single-month total ever posted. It changes only when someone finishes one month above that record. A monthly winner below the record still gets first place and the prize, but the previous record holder stays above. The reigning champion also starts at zero each month and must beat their own record to raise the number to beat.

Craps bets & payouts

The table applies standard casino resolution: line bets establish a point; Come and Don’t Come travel to their own point; place, hardway, and Big 6/8 bets remain working until they win or lose.

PASS / COME7 or 11 wins on come-out; 2, 3, 12 lose. After a point, point before 7 wins.
DON’T PASS / DON’T COME2 or 3 wins, 12 pushes; after a point, 7 before point wins.
PLACE4/10 pay 9:5; 5/9 pay 7:5; 6/8 pay 7:6. Seven loses.
HARDWAYSHard 4/10 pay 7:1; hard 6/8 pay 9:1. Seven or the easy combination loses.
FIELD3, 4, 9, 10, 11 pay even; 2 and 12 pay double.
ONE ROLL2/12 pay 30:1; 3/11 pay 15:1; Any 7 pays 4:1; Any Craps pays 7:1.
HORN / C & EHorn covers 2, 3, 11, 12. C & E combines Any Craps and Eleven.
BIG 6 / BIG 8Pays even money when its number rolls before 7.
