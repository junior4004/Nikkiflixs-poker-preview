HOW TO PLAY
Goal: play all your tiles first. Round winner scores the pips left in the other hand. First to 100 wins the match.
Opening: double 6 plays first, then double 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, blank — in that order. No doubles at all? Then 6-4 opens. No 6-4 either? Fresh shuffle.
Play: tap a glowing tile, then tap an open end if it fits both. Doubles go down sideways.
Stuck? draw from the boneyard until you can play. Boneyard empty? You must pass.
4 doubles in your opening hand? You may throw the hand in for a redeal.
Blocked (nobody can play): fewest pips wins the round and scores the difference.
