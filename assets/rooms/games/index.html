How it works

Wins, not wagersEach completed multiplayer game gives the winner one win. Bets, gold-coin totals, and winning margins never affect rank. Every player resets to zero at the start of each month—wins never roll over or combine across months.

Monthly prizeAt month-end, the player with the most wins always finishes #1 for that month and always receives 100,000 free gold coins, even when the champion banner does not change.

Single-month recordThe banner is separate from the monthly prize. It shows the highest single-month total ever posted and changes only when someone beats that record in one month. A lower total or tie leaves the banner unchanged. The reigning champion also resets to zero and must beat their own record to raise it.
