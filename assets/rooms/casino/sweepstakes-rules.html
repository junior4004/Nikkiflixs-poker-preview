Nikkiflixs Sweepstakes Rules — Redemptions OFF

1. What Nikkiflixs is

Nikkiflixs Poker Club and Entertainment runs social games — poker, slots, and table games — for entertainment. This draft covers a sweepstakes model with two separate currencies. The two currencies are never mixed up, and they are never swapped for each other.

2. The two currencies

Gold Coins — play only. Gold Coins are for playing games only. They have no cash value. They cannot be cashed out, redeemed, transferred for cash, or traded for prizes.

SC coin (Sweeps Coins) — prize redemptions only. SC coin is the sweepstakes currency in this build. SC coin is not bought directly. It is given as a bonus with Gold Coin purchases, or earned through the free entry path in section 4. SC coin can only be used to play sweepstakes games. SC coin won in sweepstakes games can be requested for prize redemption, subject to every check below — including ID check, location check, and admin approval. SC coin has no cash value until a redemption request is approved and paid.

3. Who can play

Players must be 18 or older to use the site. Open item — age by state: some states may set a higher age for this kind of product. Do not publish a single age for every state until that question is checked and approved. Players must be physically located in an allowed state at the time of play and at the time of a redemption request, and must have only one account.

4. No purchase necessary — free entry

No purchase is necessary to enter or to win. A free entry path must exist and must actually work, in plain sight, before any payout is turned on. Open item: the exact form of that path is not settled in this draft. Buying Gold Coins must never be the only way to get SC coin. Free entry requests are handled on the same terms as bonus SC coin: same games, same ID check, same location check, same admin approval.

5. How sweepstakes play works

Sweepstakes games are played with SC coin only. Gold Coin games and sweepstakes games are kept separate on screen, so a player can always tell which currency they are playing with. No purchase changes the odds of a sweepstakes game.

6. Playthrough — open item

Open item: this draft does not set a playthrough number. Until that item is settled, no redemption request can be approved on the basis of this draft alone, because the eligibility test is incomplete.

7. Prize redemption — the checks, in order

No redemption is automatic. Every request goes through all of these steps, in this order:

  1. Request. The player asks to redeem a stated amount of SC coin.
  2. Location check. Checked again at request time. A request from a blocked state is refused.
  3. ID / KYC check. Before any redemption is paid, identity is checked. A request with no completed ID check is not paid. Open item: exact documents and checking method.
  4. Account check. One account only, no fraud flags, no duplicate-account flags.
  5. Admin approval. Each payout is approved individually. Nothing auto-pays. There is no "approve all".
  6. Payment. Through PayPal only, under the ledger rules. Nikkiflixs never stores card numbers. The word "casino" is never written on any PayPal form.
  7. Ledger entry. Every approved payout is written to the payout ledger, with amount, player, date, and approver.

Kill switch: one master switch instantly stops all SC coin redemptions and payouts, while Gold Coins play-only continues. It is flipped back on only by an explicit, recorded approval — never automatically. See the build README for the full kill-switch rules.

8. Redemption limits and timing — open items

Open items: this draft does not set a minimum redemption amount, a maximum amount, a daily or monthly cap, or a payout timing promise. Those numbers must not be guessed or published until approved.

9. Fraud, duplicate accounts, and abuse

One account per person. Duplicate accounts, shared accounts, and accounts made to farm free entry are refused. Cheating, bots, game manipulation, and false ID information are refused. Nikkiflixs may pause or refuse a redemption while an account check is open, and must tell the player plainly that the check is open and what is needed to close it.

10. Location and geoblocking — draft list

Location is checked at entry and again at redemption request time. A player in a blocked state cannot play sweepstakes games and cannot redeem, even if they played from an allowed state earlier.

Draft blocked list (DRAFT ONLY — not final law, must be re-checked against official terms and current law before approval): Arizona, California, Connecticut, District of Columbia, Delaware, Idaho, Indiana, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Michigan, Mississippi, Montana, New Jersey, Nevada, New York, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Utah, Washington. New Mexico is NOT on this draft block list. Territories outside the 50 states plus DC are not settled in this draft.

11. Void where prohibited, and responsible play

These sweepstakes are void where prohibited by law. Play is for entertainment. Players should set their own limits, take breaks, and stop if play stops being fun. A responsible-play page and a way to pause or close an account must exist before payouts are turned on. Open item: exact self-exclusion wording and links.
