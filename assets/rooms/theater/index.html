The Record Caught Up

Misconduct cases where the video, the courts, and the paper trail all landed: convictions, prison time, settlements, and reform decrees — each one closed out in the record.

20 years

Walter Scott

North Charleston, South Carolina · 2015 → sentenced 2017

Officer Michael Slager shot Walter Scott during a traffic stop; a bystander filmed it. Slager is serving 20 years.

Laquan McDonald

Chicago, Illinois · 2015 → consent decree 2019

Chicago paid $5 million before any suit was filed. The dashcam video, the conviction, and the consent decree followed.

Read the full story › $1.37 million

Floyd Dent

Inkster, Michigan · 2015 → sentenced 2016

Dashcam recorded officer William Melendez punching Floyd Dent 16 times. Melendez went to prison; Inkster paid $1.37 million.

Read the full story › $3 million

Karen Garner

Loveland, Colorado · 2020 → settled 2021, sentenced 2022

The arrest of Karen Garner, an older woman with dementia, was caught on video. Loveland paid $3 million; two officers were sentenced.

Read the full story › $285,000

Levar Jones

Columbia, South Carolina · 2014

Trooper Sean Groubert shot Levar Jones during a gas-station stop; the video went viral. Jones survived — and collected $285,000.

Read the full story › $10 million

Andre Hill

Columbus, Ohio · 2020 → convicted 2024, sentenced 2025

Officer Adam Coy mistook Andre Hill’s keys and phone for a gun. Columbus paid $10 million; Coy was convicted of murder.

Read the full story › $9.5 million

Daniel Shaver

Mesa, Arizona · 2016 → acquitted 2017

Ordered to crawl down a hotel hallway, Shaver reached toward his slipping shorts and was shot five times. The jury acquitted the officer; Mesa paid $9.5 million, and he retired on a disability pension.
