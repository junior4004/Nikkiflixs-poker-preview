Independent filmmakers

Want your movie considered for the free Nikkiflixs catalog? Send a private viewing link and your film information to:

Nikkiflixs@gmail.com
  • Film title, year, runtime and synopsis
  • Your name and best contact information
  • Private Vimeo, YouTube or Drive viewing link
  • Poster, trailer and English captions if available
  • Countries where the film may be shown
  • How long Nikkiflixs may offer it
Email your submission

Please send a viewing link first—not a large movie attachment. Nikkiflixs reviews each film before anything is added, and submitting does not guarantee selection.
