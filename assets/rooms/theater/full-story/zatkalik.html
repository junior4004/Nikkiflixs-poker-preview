The $80,000 Arrest That Never Became a Lawsuit

Adams County, Colorado · February 2024 · paid pre-suit

$80,000

Paid before any lawsuit was filed — with a supervisor’s apology caught on bodycam.

What happened

In February 2024, a Colorado man was arrested for filming Adams County Deputy Walter Berlinski.

The outcome — without a lawsuit

The arrest ended in an $80,000 settlement paid pre-suit, in November 2025 — no lawsuit was ever filed. A supervisor apologized on bodycam. Whether the deputy was disciplined was not disclosed.
