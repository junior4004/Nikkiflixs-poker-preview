How to play: Classic five-card stud — the original poker. Everybody antes 10. You get one card face down (only you see it) and one card up, then a betting round. Three more up-cards follow, with a bet after each street. Bets and raises are a flat 25 (limit style, three raises max a round). The highest showing hand acts first each street — that's the one simplification from casino stud, and it's how this table runs. The club plays its own cards honestly: Pops, Scarlet and Grandma bet what they're holding, and nobody at this table can see your down card but you. Best five-card hand takes the pot, and the winner and the winning hand are named at the showdown. One friendly-table rule: a single pot, winner takes all — no side pots. Fresh deck every hand.
