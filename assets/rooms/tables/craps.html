How to playPick a chip, tap a spot to bet. Come-out roll: 7 or 11 wins the Pass Line, 2·3·12 loses. Any other number becomes the point — roll it again before a 7 to win. Come bets work like Pass Line after the point is set. Place bets pay 9:5 on 4·10, 7:5 on 5·9, 7:6 on 6·8. Hard 4·10 pay 7:1, hard 6·8 pay 9:1.
