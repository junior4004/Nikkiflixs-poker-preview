The rules of the house, in plain language. By playing at Nikkiflixs or creating an account, you agree to these terms. Last updated October 2026.

The Short Version

Free Game, Play Coins, Fair Play

  • Nikkiflixs is a free-to-play social poker club for players 18 and older. It is not a casino and no real-money gambling happens here.
  • Gold coins have no cash value. They cannot be cashed out, exchanged, or redeemed for money or prizes — ever.
  • Purchases buy digital goods inside the game (coin packages, throwables, pops, gifts). They are entertainment purchases, processed by PayPal.
  • Play fair, be decent to other players, and don't cheat. Accounts that break the fair-play rules can be suspended.
Gold Coins

What Coins Are — and Aren't

Gold coins are virtual play coins used only inside Nikkiflixs games. A coin balance is a game score. Coins are not money, not property, and not a store of value: they cannot be withdrawn, transferred to another person for value, sold, or redeemed for cash or prizes of any kind. If the service ever ends, coin balances end with it.

Purchases

Buying Digital Goods

The store sells digital goods that are delivered and used inside the game: gold coin packages, Pops and throwable items, gifts, and other table extras. All payments are processed by PayPal; your card and bank details go to PayPal and never to us. Prices are shown before you buy, in the store. Digital goods are delivered to your account or device as soon as the payment completes.

If a purchase goes wrong — you were charged and didn't receive your goods, or something was billed twice — contact us and we will fix it or help you through PayPal's buyer protection. Buying is always optional: nothing in the store changes how the cards are dealt or gives a player an edge in a hand.

Accounts

Your Account, Your Password

  • One account per person. Pick a username that doesn't impersonate someone else or break the conduct rules below.
  • You're responsible for activity on your account — keep your password to yourself. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes; we cannot recover or read them.
  • We may suspend or close accounts that cheat, harass other players, or abuse the service.
Fair Play

How Players Are Expected to Behave

  • No cheating — no exploiting bugs for gain, no collusion against other players in multiplayer games, no automated scripts or software playing on your account.
  • No harassment — table talk and throwables are part of the fun; threats, hate speech, and targeted abuse are not.
  • Found a bug? Tell us through Contact instead of using it — we fix bugs and we remember who reported them kindly.

How the deal itself stays honest is on the Fairness page, including the statistical test we ran on the shuffle.
