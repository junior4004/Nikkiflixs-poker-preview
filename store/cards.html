How Our Prices Work

STEP 1

Real Sold Listings

Every card is looked up on eBay's sold listings — completed sales, real money that changed hands. Asking prices don't count.

STEP 2

The Six-Price Average

We take six recent solds — the 2 highest, the 2 lowest, and 2 from the middle — and average them. Highs and lows cancel out. That average is the price.

STEP 3

Know Your Bin

Every card lives in a numbered bin, 1 to 2,000. The bin number on a listing is exactly where that card sits — the card you see is the card you get.
