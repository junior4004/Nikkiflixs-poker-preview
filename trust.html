Trust & Safety

The plain facts about who runs Nikkiflixs, how your payments and account are handled, and what we never collect — so you can check us out before you spend a cent.

Who We Are

An Independent US Company

Nikkiflixs Poker Club and Entertainment is an independent entertainment company based in Alamogordo, New Mexico, USA, established in 2026. We are a small operation and we say so openly — everything on this page is written by us, about our own site, and every claim below is one you can check against how the site actually works.

What This Is

A Social Poker Club, Not a Casino

Nikkiflixs is a social poker club and entertainment site: multiplayer poker tables, a movie theater, a radio room, and free sports information. Playing is free. Purchases — gold coin packages and table extras — are optional, and exist for players who want a bigger stack or more toys at the table.

Nikkiflixs is not a real-money casino. No real-money gambling takes place here.

Gold Coins

Coins Have No Cash Value

Gold coins are virtual entertainment coins used only inside Nikkiflixs games. They cannot be cashed out, cannot be exchanged or traded for money, and cannot be redeemed for prizes of value. A coin balance is a game score, nothing more — treat any purchase as buying entertainment, the same as tokens at an arcade.

Payments

Every Payment Runs Through PayPal

Every purchase on this site is processed by PayPal. When you buy, your card and bank details go directly to PayPal and never touch our servers — we never see them, and we could not leak what we never hold. This is the same checkout that processed our first real sale, completed on October 20, 2026.

If you ever have a payment problem, you also have PayPal's own buyer protections on top of ours — and you can always reach us first at the contact below.

Account Security

How Your Account Is Protected

  • HTTPS everywhere — the whole site is served over encrypted TLS connections.
  • Passwords are hashed, never stored — account passwords are kept only as salted, one-way scrypt hashes. Nobody at Nikkiflixs can read your password, and neither can anyone who ever saw our database.
  • Sessions are hashed too — the sign-in tokens that keep you logged in are stored only as SHA-256 hashes.
  • Login attempts are throttled — repeated failed sign-ins are rate-limited, which slows down anyone trying to guess a password.
  • Managed database — account data lives in a managed Postgres database (Neon), not on a hobby server under a desk.
Your Information

What We Never Collect

We never ask for, collect, or store:

  • Card numbers or card security codes
  • Bank account or bank login details
  • Government ID numbers

An account here needs a username and a password you choose. That is the whole list.

The Honest Part

Small Company, Straight Answers

We are a small, independent company, and we would rather state plainly how we work than dress it up. No website can promise perfect security — what we can tell you is where the sensitive data goes: payments go to PayPal, passwords are stored only as hashes, and the rest is a free game with optional coins. If any of that ever changes, this page changes with it.
