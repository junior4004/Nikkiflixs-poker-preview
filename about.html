About Nikkiflixs

A social poker club and entertainment site — free to play, run by a small independent company in the USA, and proud to say exactly what it is and what it isn't.

What This Is

A Poker Club & Entertainment, Not a Casino

Nikkiflixs Poker Club and Entertainment is a free-to-play social club: multiplayer poker tables, blackjack, craps and five-card stud, slot machines, a game room, a lounge with pool, shuffleboard and darts, a radio room, and a movie theater showing our own films and documentaries.

There is no real-money gambling here. Gold coins are play coins — a score, not a currency. They cannot be cashed out or redeemed for anything of value, and no game on this site pays out money or prizes. Because no gambling takes place, Nikkiflixs holds no gambling license — there is nothing here that one would apply to. We would rather tell you that plainly than borrow the appearance of credentials that don't fit what this is.

Who Runs It

An Independent US Company, Est. 2026

Nikkiflixs is run by Nikkiflixs Poker Club and Entertainment, an independent entertainment company based in the USA and established in 2026. We are a small operation, not a corporation with a call center — the same people who built this site answer its email.

The same business runs public pages under the same name, including our YouTube channel — home of our film series The Heavyweight Debt and our true-crime documentaries — and our Facebook page, and the business is listed on Google. If a page claims to be us and isn't reachable from here or from those channels, it isn't us.

The Rooms

What's Inside

  • Poker Room — multiplayer Texas Hold'em and Omaha tables, hosted by Nikki.
  • Tables — blackjack, craps, and five-card stud.
  • Casino Floor — slot machines and the coin store. (The name is a theme, not a business model: everything runs on play coins.)
  • Game Room — dominoes and more table games.
  • Theater — our films and full-story documentaries.
  • Radio & Nikki's Bar — music, pool, shuffleboard, and darts.
How It Makes Money

Optional Purchases, Nothing Hidden

Playing is free. The club supports itself with optional purchases — gold coin packages and table extras like throwables and gifts — all processed by PayPal. Buying is never required to play, to win a hand, or to be treated fairly at a table. Our Fairness page explains how the cards are dealt and the test we ran on the shuffle.

Entertainment Only

Responsible, Social Play

Nikkiflixs is entertainment for an adult (18+) audience. There is no money to win and no money to lose at the tables — the worst outcome of a bad night here is a smaller pile of play coins. Play for the fun of the game, take breaks, and stop when it stops being fun. If you ever feel a purchase wasn't worth it, that's a signal to stop buying — the free game is the whole game, and it isn't going anywhere.
