Privacy Policy

What information Nikkiflixs uses, where it lives, and what we never do with it — written to match how the site actually works. Last updated October 2026.

On Your Device

Your Game Lives in Your Browser

Most of your Nikkiflixs life never leaves your phone or computer. Your gold coin wallet, the items you own (throwables, pops, gifts, dice sets), your display name and profile choices, and your game preferences are kept in your browser's local storage — a small storage area on your own device. We don't receive copies of it. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, the same as clearing any game's save file.

Accounts

If You Create an Account

An account needs a username and a password you choose — nothing else. On our server, your password is stored only as a salted, one-way scrypt hash; nobody at Nikkiflixs can read it, including us. The tokens that keep you signed in are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, and sign-in attempts are rate-limited. Account data lives in a managed Postgres database (Neon). Your username is what other players see at the table.

Payments

PayPal Handles the Money

Every purchase is processed by PayPal. Your card and bank details go directly to PayPal and never touch our site or servers. We never ask for, collect, or store card numbers, card security codes, bank logins, or government ID numbers — see the full list on our Trust & Safety page.

Tracking

No Ad Trackers. No Data Business.

This site runs no advertising trackers and no analytics pixels — no Google Analytics, no Facebook pixel, nothing that follows you around the web or builds an advertising profile of you. We do not sell personal information to anyone, and we don't share it for marketing.

Two honest exceptions, both about services you can see: the site's fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives the font request like any web font service; and films in the Theater are embedded from YouTube, so when you press play on a video, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that playback.

Email

If You Write to Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you wrote. We use it to answer you and to sort out the issue you raised — nothing else. We don't add you to marketing lists you didn't ask for.

Uploader

Nikkiflixs Uploader

The Nikkiflixs Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Nikkiflixs (@jerrytuter2902). It uploads video files the owner provides, adds them to the owner's own playlists, and updates details on the owner's own videos. Through Google sign-in, it accesses only what is needed for those uploads. It stores a sign-in token on the operator's own computer so it can stay signed in. It collects nothing from viewers or subscribers, and it never sells, shares, or transfers any data to anyone.

Changes

If This Ever Changes

If we ever change what we collect or why, this page changes with it and the update date moves. Questions about your information, or a request about your account data: contact us.
